For nib members: We are a preferred nib provider, but not a no-gap clinic. A gap may apply depending on your cover, limits and treatment. Please check with nib if you are unsure.

Government dental programs

Child Dental Benefits Schedule

Eligible children may access bulk-billed basic dental services under the CDBS, up to a capped amount set by Services Australia. Eligibility, capped amounts and available balances can change — ask our team or visit servicesaustralia.gov.au for current details.

Veterans’ Affairs

Eligible DVA cardholders may access clinically necessary dental services. Treatment limits or co-payments may apply.
