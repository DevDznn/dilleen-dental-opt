Our Services

Complete dental care for every smile

From routine family dentistry to implants and complete smile rehabilitation, our Rockhampton team provides personalised care from one convenient Archer Street location.

Family Dentistry

Routine dental examinations, professional cleans, preventive care and digital X-rays for patients of every age. Please note that 3D OPG imaging is not available onsite.

Learn more →

Dental Implants

Personalised implant options for missing teeth, including single-tooth implants, implant-supported bridges and implant-retained dentures.

Learn more →

Crowns, Veneers & Bridges

Custom porcelain veneers, crowns and bridges made by trusted dental laboratories. We do not use an onsite CEREC® system, so the usual laboratory turnaround is approximately two weeks.

Learn more →

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

A carefully planned combination of treatments designed to rebuild damaged or missing teeth and improve your smile's comfort, appearance and function.

Learn more →

Teeth Whitening

Brighten your smile with professionally supervised in-chair whitening or convenient take-home whitening kits.

Learn more →

Invisalign

Gradually straighten your teeth with a personalised series of discreet, comfortable and removable clear aligners.

Learn more →

Root Canal Therapy

Treatment for infected or inflamed teeth focused on relieving discomfort and preserving your natural tooth whenever possible.

Learn more →

Tooth-Coloured Fillings

Natural-looking composite fillings carefully colour-matched and shaped to restore teeth affected by decay, wear or minor damage.

Learn more →

Sports Mouthguards

Custom-fitted mouthguards designed to provide secure, comfortable protection for your teeth during contact and collision sports.

About Us

Who we are

Dilleen Dental is a QIP accredited dental practice that has been located in South Rockhampton since 1995. Our welcoming team of dentists and support staff are here to provide you with the best dental care possible.

We care for the dental needs of the whole family — and we're always open to welcoming new patients.

Eligible children may access up to $1,000 in basic dental services over two consecutive calendar years. Eligibility and available balances apply.
