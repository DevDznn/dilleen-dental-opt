Appointments

Booking & visiting

Do you accept new patients?

Yes. Dilleen Dental welcomes new patients of all ages, including individuals and families in the Rockhampton region.

What are your opening hours?

Dilleen Dental is open Monday to Wednesday and Friday from 8:00am to 5:00pm, and Thursday from 8:00am to 5:30pm. The clinic is closed Saturday and Sunday.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online through our appointment page or call Dilleen Dental on (07) 4922 3039.

What should I do if I have an urgent dental concern?

Call the clinic directly on (07) 4922 3039 so our team can discuss your concern and advise you about current appointment availability.

Do you accept private health funds?

Yes. Dilleen Dental accepts all private health funds and provides CDBS bulk billing for eligible children. Read our new-patient information for more details.

Our practice

Trust & accreditation

Is Dilleen Dental accredited?

Yes. Dilleen Dental is a QIP-accredited dental practice.

How long has Dilleen Dental been operating?

Dilleen Dental has cared for the South Rockhampton community since 1995.

Who will I see for my appointment?

Our dental team includes Dr Priyanka Rathi, Dr Hitesh Rathi, Dr Ryan Chen and Dr Chris Xu. Your appointment details will identify the dentist selected for your visit. Meet everyone on our team page.

Where is Dilleen Dental located?

Dilleen Dental is at 80 Archer Street, Rockhampton QLD 4700, on the corner of Archer and Campbell Streets, across from Lifeline.

Please call our team before your visit for the latest parking, entry and accessibility information.

What areas does Dilleen Dental serve?
