Your first visit

A calm start, shaped around you.

Joining a new dental practice is easier when you know what to expect. Your first appointment begins with a conversation about your concerns, dental history and goals.

Depending on your individual needs, the visit may include an examination and any clinically appropriate assessment. Your dentist will explain their findings and discuss suitable next steps, giving you time to ask questions before deciding how to proceed.

  1. 01

    Tell us what matters

    Share any concerns, symptoms, past experiences or goals you would like your dentist to understand.

  2. 02

    Receive an individual assessment

    Your dentist will recommend the assessment that is appropriate for your oral health and circumstances.

  3. 03

    Understand your next steps

    We explain findings and options clearly so you can make an informed decision about your care.

Before your appointment

A few useful things to bring.

If something is unavailable, do not let that stop you from contacting us. Our team can explain what is relevant for your appointment.

  • ✓
    Health fund details

    Your current membership card or digital membership details, if applicable.

  • ✓
    Medicare or CDBS details

    Bring the relevant information if you are asking about eligibility for a child.

  • ✓
    Medical information

    A current list of medications and any health information your dentist should know.

  • ✓
    Existing dental records

    Referral letters or recent dental images if you already have them; ask our team if you are unsure.

Cover and claiming

Health funds and CDBS.

We keep the information simple and encourage you to confirm the details that apply to your own cover before treatment.

Private health funds

Dilleen Dental accepts all private health funds. Bring your current membership details and ask our team about the claiming options available for your appointment.

CDBS for eligible children

Dilleen Dental provides bulk billing for eligible children covered by the Child Dental Benefits Schedule. Eligibility and available benefits should be confirmed before treatment.

Payment information

Costs and any out-of-pocket amount depend on the care you need and your individual cover. Our team can explain available payment options and provide relevant information before you proceed.

Common new-patient questions.

Do you accept new patients?

Yes. Dilleen Dental welcomes new patients of all ages, including individuals and families in the Rockhampton region.

Do you accept private health funds?

Yes. Dilleen Dental accepts all private health funds. Bring your current membership details and ask our team about the claiming options available for your appointment.

Is CDBS bulk billing available for my child?

Dilleen Dental provides CDBS bulk billing for eligible children. Contact the clinic before the appointment so the relevant eligibility and benefit details can be checked.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

If relevant, bring your health fund or Medicare details, a list of medications and any recent referral or dental records you already have. Call us if you are unsure what applies to you.
