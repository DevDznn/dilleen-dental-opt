Who we are

Part of the Rockhampton community since 1995.

Dilleen Dental is a QIP-accredited dental practice located in South Rockhampton.

Our welcoming team of dentists and support staff is here to provide you with the best dental care possible. We care for the dental needs of the whole family and are always happy to welcome new patients.

Our clinic accepts all health fund members, as well as Veterans’ Affairs cardholders. We also provide bulk billing for eligible children covered under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS).
